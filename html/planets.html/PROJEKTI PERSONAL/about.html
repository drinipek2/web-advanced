About This Website

Welcome to Solar System Explorer! This project was envisioned and created by Drini to bring the wonders of our beautiful cosmic neighborhood right to your screen. The primary goal of this website is to provide students, educators, and curious visitors with an engaging, user-friendly platform to learn fascinating facts about the planets. From understanding their planetary age and unique atmospheric compositions to measuring their massive distances from the Sun, this site serves as an accessible gateway to the stars.



The website uses HTML and CSS to create a responsive and interactive experience. Images and educational information were gathered from reliable scientific sources.
